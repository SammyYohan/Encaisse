Les paiements en ligne sont traités par Stripe (carte bancaire, SEPA, ACH). L'éditeur ne conserve aucune donnée de carte bancaire. Online payments are processed by Stripe (card, SEPA, ACH). The publisher stores no card data. The software generates PDFs with the country's mandatory details (legal name, address, tax ID, due date). Users must have their templates and rates validated by their accountant.

The service is provided "as is". Data stored locally (~5 MB): if the device is lost, stolen or the browser cleared without an encrypted backup or JSON/CSV export, history is lost. Privacy policy

1. What we collect

  • On your device: your clients, quotes, invoices, credit notes, site photos and settings stay in your browser's local storage. They never leave the device, except for the explicit cases below.
  • Shared customer pages: only when you tap Share / Payment link / Remind, a copy of the document (including customer contact details) is published to our Cloudflare D1 database to display the /r/ page to the recipient.
  • Online backup: if you enable it, a copy encrypted on your device (AES-GCM) is stored on Cloudflare D1. Neither Cloudflare nor the publisher can read it; only your recovery key (kept by you) can decrypt it.
  • For payment: name, e-mail and amount sent to Stripe when you subscribe or get paid. Stripe acts as a payment subprocessor.
  • For e-mails: recipient address, subject and text sent to Brevo for a reminder or receipt. Brevo acts as a sending subprocessor.
  • Technical logs: Cloudflare access logs (IP, browser) kept for security and fraud prevention.

2. No resale, no ad trackers

Encaisse contains no advertising tracker, no data resale and no profiling. The JSON and CSV exports are the official way to obtain all of your data.

3. Retention periods

  • Device: as long as you keep the app and its data (export to JSON/CSV regularly and enable the encrypted backup).
  • Customer pages and backups (Cloudflare D1): while the link is active; deleted on simple request to privacy@encaisse.app.
  • Payments (Stripe) and e-mails (Brevo): per their own retention policies, for the duration of accounting and tax obligations.
  • Technical logs (Cloudflare): 12 months maximum.

Lawful bases (GDPR): performance of the contract (service, subscription, requested e-mails), legitimate interest (security, fraud and abuse prevention).
